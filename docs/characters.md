# Night Shift — Characters

Everyone in the facility on the night of the incident, plus the police detective who arrives in hour 9. The story is in [story.md](story.md), and gameplay is in [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md).

**Status:** the **roles** are decided. The **names, personalities and story roles** are suggestions, not decided. Badge IDs are still to be assigned.

Players only ever see text, so every character needs a **distinctive way of speaking**. That's how players learn to recognise people by their badge ID, and it's what makes the Entity's tells stand out. Remember the Entity's tells when writing anyone it possesses: it says "Indeed.", never uses contractions, nicknames or abbreviations, and is very literal. **No other character ever says "indeed".**

## The night shift

### 1. Raymond Okafor — night security supervisor

- **Background:** ex-military. He's run night security here for years and treats it as a post to be held. Calm in a crisis, and expects everyone else to be too.
- **Voice:** short, clipped sentences. Strict radio procedure every time: call signs and "over". Calls people by their surnames: "Kowalski", "Webb".
- **Verbal tics:** "Copy." "Understood." "Say again?"
- **Story roles:** carries a radio. Sends Danny on patrol in hour 1. Leads the response to the lockdown. In hour 3 he stops Margaret leaving through the Entrance, and in the Security office the Entity **jumps into him** (decided). In hour 4 the Entity takes him around the facility, then jumps into Marcus. In hour 6 he and Marcus try to work out what's happened, with gaps in both their memories. In hour 8 he leads the confrontation with Jamal, and **shoots him** (decided). He never knows that Jamal was innocent, and that he was a carrier himself. **When he shoots Jamal, the Entity jumps into him** (decided). In hour 9 he's the **last person the detective interviews**, and the Entity jumps from him into the detective in the final lines of the game (decided). Alternates patrols with Danny. Suggested as the person who searches the Security office with the carrier after the murder.
- **Notes:** when the Entity takes him, his radio procedure slips, which is a strong tell for anyone who knows his habits. His use of surnames is close to the Entity's formality, so the difference has to be clear: he still uses contractions.

### 2. Danny Kowalski — night guard

- **Background:** in his twenties, and in the job because it was available. Friendly, restless, and popular with everyone. Struggling more than he lets on.
- **Voice:** slang, swearing, filler words, and a nickname for everyone: "Ray", "Doc", "Lils".
- **Verbal tics:** "Dude." "No way." "It's fine, it's fine."
- **Story roles:** carries a radio. Patrols on alternate hours, leaving at quarter past (decided). **Late** for his hour 1 patrol. In hour 3, Luis finds him on patrol and tells him about the lost badge. Has the **drug problem** (Secret 5). When Luis loses his badge, Danny **agrees not to report it** (decided). **Makes the visitor badge** for the delivery driver (decided: the night guard does this).
- **Notes:** he did Luis a favour by not reporting the lost badge, so he can ask Luis to return it when Luis finds out about the drugs. Write him sympathetically.

### 3. Dr. Margaret Ashworth — senior scientist

- **Background:** the most senior scientist, decades in the field. Dry, precise, impatient with nonsense, and quietly worried about Artifact-01.
- **Voice:** formal and exact, but she does use contractions.
- **Verbal tics:** "I'd rather we didn't guess." "Let's be precise about this." She never says "indeed": only the Entity does.
- **Story roles:** in hour 1 she's in the **Observation post** with Priya. In hour 2, alone after Priya leaves for coffee, she sees the Vault door open, hits the **emergency lockdown**, then hears a noise, goes into the Vault and becomes the Entity's **first carrier** (decided). In hour 3 the Entity tries to leave through the Entrance in her, then jumps from her into Raymond in the Security office. She's left with a memory gap.
- **Notes:** she uses contractions like everyone else, so the change when the Entity takes her is noticeable to a careful player. As the senior scientist, people defer to her, which gives the Entity perfect cover.

### 4. Dr. Priya Nair — scientist

- **Background:** a younger researcher with a reputation for being right before anyone else. Studies the Compass.
- **Voice:** fast and technical. She thinks out loud, and often leaves sentences unfinished as she jumps to the next idea.
- **Verbal tics:** "Okay, okay, okay." "Hang on." "That doesn't make sense unless..."
- **Story roles:** suggested as the **Compass holder** (Secret 6), hunting the Entity through the facility.
- **Notes:** she's the first to suspect something is moving between people. She's always one step behind the Entity.

### 5. Dr. Owen Reyes — scientist

- **Background:** warm and well liked, with a partner and two kids he talks about constantly.
- **Voice:** relaxed, lots of jokes and small talk.
- **Verbal tics:** "Ha!" "Did I tell you what my youngest did?"
- **Story roles:** in hour 1 he's in a lab with Lily, **winding the Music Box for the first time**, without activating it (decided). Suggested as the **murdered scientist** (Secret 8). His badge opens the Vault, or another door the Entity needs.
- **Notes:** the player should like him before he goes silent. His small talk is also what the Entity can't do, so a conversation with him and a carrier highlights the tells.

### 6. Lily Chen — intern

- **Background:** started **earlier this week**. Eager to impress, nervous, and doesn't yet know the rules or the people.
- **Voice:** polite, apologetic, full of questions.
- **Verbal tics:** "Sorry, can I ask...?" "Is that normal?" "Oh! Okay."
- **Story roles:** in hour 1 she's in a lab with Owen, **winding the Music Box for the first time**, without activating it (decided). A natural listener for explanations and retellings.
- **Notes:** people explain things to her, which gives the player background without heavy exposition. Nobody knows her well enough to notice when she suddenly stops asking questions.

### 7. Luis Herrera — cleaner

- **Background:** has cleaned the facility for years, and knows it room by room. Quiet, observant, and a good friend to Danny.
- **Voice:** short and dry. Says little, notices everything.
- **Verbal tics:** "Mm." "I saw." "Not my business."
- **Story roles:** **loses his badge in the men's bathroom** in **hour 3** (Secret 9, decided), and tells Danny while Danny is on patrol. He knows he had it when he came in, because he used it to get into a restricted area. Danny agrees not to report it, but Luis has to find it. Suggested as **the friend** who discovers Danny's drug problem (Secret 5), perhaps while searching the bathroom for his badge.
- **Notes:** "Not my business" is the struggle at the heart of his story. He owes Danny for keeping the badge quiet, which makes it even harder to report him.

### 8. Bev Price — cleaner

- **Background:** twenty years at the facility. Knows everyone, and everything about everyone.
- **Voice:** long, rambling and warm, full of gossip.
- **Verbal tics:** pet names: "love", "sweetheart", "pet". "Don't tell anyone I told you, but..."
- **Story roles:** passes on gossip and background about the others, and overhears things.
- **Notes:** her pet names are the opposite of the Entity's "no nicknames" tell. If she ever turns formal, players should notice.

### 9. Marcus Webb — IT lead

- **Background:** runs IT on nights, and reports to the player character. Overworked, sarcastic, and loyal to the player.
- **Voice:** heavy contractions, jargon, abbreviations and nicknames: "the box" for the security server, "Ray", "Doc".
- **Verbal tics:** "Classic." "Have you tried turning it off and on again?" "Not my department."
- **Story roles:** in hour 4, the Entity jumps from Raymond into him, and he goes to the Server room and **takes down the system**: the video is lost and corrupted, and the user logs are wiped (decided). The Entity leaves him in hour 5. In hour 6 he investigates the damage with Raymond, not knowing he caused it. Carries a **radio**: Raymond calls him on it in hour 6 (decided).
- **Notes:** possessed, his speech changes completely: "the security server", not "the box". That change is the clearest proof he wasn't himself, and the player has a personal stake in clearing him.

## Others in the building

### 10. Dr. Richard Voss — facility director

- **Background:** runs the facility and answers to whoever funds it. **Working late.** In hour 1 he's in **his office**, on the **phone** with someone outside the facility (decided), possibly about Artifact-01, which arrived recently.
- **Voice:** polished and careful. Corporate euphemisms, never specific: "the situation", "the incident", "the item".
- **Verbal tics:** "Let's not get ahead of ourselves." "I'll handle it." "That's above your clearance."
- **Story roles:** the most senior person in the building, so everyone looks to him during the lockdown. Suggested secret: he pushed to bring Artifact-01 in quickly and cut corners on its containment, and he wants that kept quiet.
- **Notes:** he's a natural suspect, because he's hiding something. His secret is about the past, not about what's happening tonight. He may know more about Artifact-01 than anyone else. His phone calls only record his half of the conversation, unless he uses the speaker.

### 11. Gus Brennan — maintenance supervisor

- **Background:** old-school maintenance man, decades at the facility. Knows every pipe and duct. **Working late on the HVAC system.**
- **Voice:** gruff and folksy, full of sayings. Talks to the machines as if they're people.
- **Verbal tics:** "She's had it." "Right as rain." "Come on, old girl."
- **Story roles:** his crew's HVAC work **accidentally shorts the security doors** in hour 2, opening the Vault and starting the whole incident (decided). In hour 7 he discovers Jamal trying to clear the electrical jam, and **Jamal, possessed by the Entity, kills him**. His body is left in the Facilities room (decided). Suggested as the person who **knows the vents** best (Secret 3).
- **Notes:** the player should like him before he dies, so his folksy voice needs room in the early hours. His sayings are perfect against the Entity's literalness. Possessed, he'd stop using them; and if someone says one of his sayings to a carrier, the carrier takes it literally.

### 12. Jamal Boateng — maintenance technician

- **Background:** Gus's technician, a couple of years into the job. Easy-going, good with his hands, and small enough to fit into the ducts.
- **Voice:** relaxed and friendly. Always has music in his ears.
- **Verbal tics:** hums or sings along to his music, which the AI tags as `[HUMMING]`. "No worries." "On it, boss."
- **Story roles:** works on the HVAC system with Gus when the short happens (decided). Later, the Entity **jumps into him** because he understands the electrical fault that keeps the lockdown going. Possessed, he **kills Gus** when Gus finds him, then **clears the jam**, opening the way towards the Loading dock (all in hour 7, decided). In hour 8 he's confronted and **shot by Raymond**, and the scientists activate Artifact-01 to pull the Entity back in (decided). The one who'd actually crawl through the vents. Suggested as someone who uses vent travel (Secret 3) during the night.
- **Notes:** his humming is a small red herring next to the Music Box's `[MUSIC]`. As a former carrier, he won't remember killing Gus, which makes his discovery of what he did one of the darkest moments in the story. Write him as likeable and gentle, so the murder feels completely out of character.

### 13. Earl Dobbs — delivery driver

- **Background:** a long-haul trucker on his last drop of the night. Fell asleep in his truck at the Loading dock, and woke up to the lockdown alarms. **Not staff and has no badge.** Just wants to go home.
- **Voice:** trucker talk and folksy rambling. Easily scared, and says so.
- **Verbal tics:** CB radio slang: "10-4", "good buddy", "breaker breaker". "I just drop off the boxes."
- **Story roles:** the outsider. The AI gives his voice a **random ID**, and at first he isn't on the map (decided). Later, the **night guard makes him a visitor badge** with its **own number** (decided), so from then on his voice ID and his badge ID never match. People have to explain things to him, and he asks what the player is wondering.
- **Notes:** his "10-4" and CB talk are a red herring for the radio secret. He sounds like he's on a radio, but he isn't.

### 14. Det. Frank Mercer — police detective

*Suggestion. The role is decided; the name and personality aren't.*

- **Background:** a homicide detective called in after the SWAT team secures the building. Decades on the job, calm, hard to rattle. Has no idea what the facility really does.
- **Voice:** patient and plain-spoken. Asks simple questions and lets people talk. Uses plenty of contractions and casual phrases, so the change is obvious when the Entity takes him.
- **Verbal tics:** "Take your time." "Walk me through it." "Okay. And then what?"
- **Story roles:** only appears in **hour 9**, interviewing everyone room by room (decided). The **Entity jumps into him** at the end of Raymond's interview, the last log in the game (decided).
- **Notes:**
  - He has **no badge**, so the AI gives his voice a **random ID**, like the delivery driver's.
  - His habit of asking people to walk him through things is the opposite of the Entity's literal, clipped answers, so the final lines can show the switch clearly.
  - **The game ends on his line "Indeed."** (decided). A possible lead-up: Raymond, suddenly himself again, says "Sorry, what were we talking about?", and the detective answers "Nothing of importance. I think we are finished here." Then: "Indeed."
